## 2026-10-28 P04 correction: Submit is not proof of terminality

**Supersedes the earlier one-page assumption wherever it appears above.** The operator corrected the product requirement: a form may label an intermediate **Next/Continue/Submit** button **Submit**, then reveal additional application questions. The earlier code's label-specific `expectedSubmitText` and same-path assumption were insufficient. Do not regress to identifying terminal effects by button wording. Do not force a one-page fixture to stand in for the real workflow.

**Canonical state model:** `DISARMED → ARMED → PENDING → IN_PROGRESS (another question step, repeated as necessary) → PROCESSING (explicit pending employer processing message) → CONFIRMED (explicit received/submitted receipt)`. `VALIDATION_FAILED` and `BLOCKED` are distinct interruption states. A user can retry a validation failure. Only `CONFIRMED` writes a durable receipt or updates Applied, even if the employer says "application being processed." Preserve `PROCESSING` as visibly observed but non-final; it does not by itself prove successful acceptance.

**Source authority:** `artifacts/escape-hatch/managed-submission-observer.ts` owns the read-only reducer and page binding. `managed-listeners.ts` owns allowlisted provider-specific form controls and same-job descendants. `src/components/managed-browser-assist.tsx` projects the state. `managed-submission-observer.test.ts` and `managed-listeners.test.ts` prove multi-step continuity plus document and receipt status. Provider contracts/Issue #69 remain parents. Never construct a competing observer.

**Recurrent failure guardrails:**
- A label, click, Enter, URL change, disappearing button, final-looking headline quoted in a posting, form filled or rendered stage transition cannot equal `CONFIRMED`.
- A **verified same-origin descendant of the bound exact job path** may carry more questions, retaining the same observer and opportunity identity. Another job path, origin or unrecognized redirect must fail closed for review, not silently become the same application.
- An exact employer-confirmation message must be visible, unique in a success-only adapter scope, post-attempt, with **no remaining actionable application questions or form-submit controls**. Reject preexisting messages, generic Thanks, ambiguous or mixed validation errors, and forged page-script events.
- Submission observer is **passive**. It never clicks, advances, attests, signs or submits. The user owns terminal Submit; other automated navigation remains under the independent trusted-effect policy.
- Status and local durable receipt are distinct: processing is not Applied, provider tracker sync is not evidence that a click succeeded, and a page confirmation is not live-observed from CI.
- Future adapters should declare explicit host, job scope, continuation scope and verified success containers rather than assume uniform ATS page shapes. Multi-page fallback with unrecognized path is a review gate, not evidence loss or automatic receipt.
